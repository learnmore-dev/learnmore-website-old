# SEO Maintenance & Search Engine Optimization Plan

**Project:** Learn More Technologies  
**Domain:** `https://learnmoretechnologies.in`  

---

## 🔍 Recurring SEO Audit Schedule

### Weekly SEO Checks:
1. **Google Search Console Coverage:** Review 'Indexed' vs 'Not Indexed' URLs; resolve any 404, redirect error, or canonical mismatch.
2. **Crawl Stats & Sitemaps:** Confirm `/sitemap.xml` status is 'Success' with 42 submitted URLs.
3. **Core Keyword Tracking:** Track Google SERP rank for primary terms:
   - "software training institute in bangalore"
   - "python full stack training in marathahalli"
   - "aws certification training in btm layout"
   - "data science course in bangalore with placement"

### Monthly SEO Checks:
1. **Structured Data Validation:** Test sample course, blog, and location pages with Google Rich Results Test tool.
2. **Internal Link Health:** Run internal link crawler to ensure zero 404 or broken links.
3. **Robots.txt Integrity:** Verify `robots.txt` remains accessible and AI crawler disallow rules remain active.
4. **Organic Traffic Analysis:** Review Google Analytics 4 organic search landing page conversion rates.
